❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I make a custom iPhone ringtone from YouTube?

Paste any YouTube music link into SnapCut, trim your favorite 20-30 second chorus, download the MP3, and set it via GarageBand or iTunes as your iPhone ringtone.

Can I set downloaded clips as an Android ringtone?

Yes! Android directly supports MP3 files. Once downloaded from SnapCut, go to Settings -> Sound & Vibration -> Phone Ringtone and select the MP3.

What is the recommended length for a phone ringtone?

The ideal ringtone length is 20 to 30 seconds. This gives your phone enough time to ring through a full chorus loop without cutting off abruptly.
